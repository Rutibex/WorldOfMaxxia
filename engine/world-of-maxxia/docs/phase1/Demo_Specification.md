# World of Maxxia — Phase 1: Demo Specification and Interaction Contract
Version 1.0 · Design baseline · No executable game is included in Phase 1.

## 1. Product and authority
A fast, single-player, first-person party blobber in Maxxia. Six adventurers explore a colourful town and two dungeon floors, fight exaggerated animated monsters, recover strange treasures and return to town to improve. Mouse and keyboard operate the same permanent fantasy dashboard. Target 2–4 hours for a first completion; playtesting, rather than map size alone, determines duration.

User decisions override this document. The agreed 34-phase plan governs delivery. The attached Hexmaster Classic draft supplies class identities and six abilities; it does not dictate computer-game mechanics. The supplied Xeen report, manual and screenshots supply interface references. This specification defines World of Maxxia behavior wherever fidelity is uncertain. Do not describe these new formulas or proposed local lore as existing Hexmaster canon or original Xeen mechanics.

Confirmed setting anchors: Maxxia is the user's setting; Malidrex is the witch/alchemist narrator associated with Hexmaster; the six class identities are Myrmidon/STR, Artificer/DEX, Mutant/CON, Wizard/INT, Priest/WIS, Witch/CHA. The local town, NPCs, plot, enemy variants, items and spell names below are NEW DEMO LORE, not established facts from the rulebook. Keep them in a self-contained district without defining Maxxia's global geography, governments or cosmology. Spell entries are new computer-game adaptations; no exact tabletop equivalence is claimed.

## 2. Non-negotiable play principles
- Attack is one committed action. No maneuver picker, combo sequence or scripting language.
- Noncaster specialties are automatic attacks/passives configured outside combat. Useful defaults require no setup.
- All three casters use one SP system and one spell UI. Their unique lists provide their identities. No Vancian preparation, priest Circles, mutation rolls or gem casting costs.
- Six active slots maximum; at least one living active adventurer required to leave the tavern. Default is one of each class. Duplicate classes allowed.
- Four-facing tile movement; discrete turns and strafing. No free camera, tactical battle board or individually positioned party units.
- Combat and travel share the world view. Menus do not advance time. The game waits for deliberate commands.
- Finished demo uses original full-colour pixel art, animated enemies/NPC scenes, sound and music. Temporary implementation assets are tracked and removed before release.

## 3. Bounded scope and release gate
Three 16×16 maps: town Bellwether Quay, dungeon Upper Cistern and dungeon Resonator Vault. Names are new demo lore. Dungeon entrance is in town; no separate wilderness is required. Eight service/quest NPCs, eight ordinary enemy types and one boss; twelve selectable base portraits with condition treatments; 36 class-exclusive spells; 40 ordinary item definitions and eight notable rewards; six main stages and three optional quests; levels 1–5. Map size includes solid/inaccessible tiles and is a content budget, not a demand to fill every cell.

Included: creation, roster, combat, spells, equipment, loot, shops, banking, training, healing, rest/food/light, locks/traps/secrets, automap, journal, time, settings, manual/autosaves and portable saves. Excluded: multiplayer/accounts/cloud saves, cloud video infrastructure, full wilderness, mounts, strongholds, procedural campaign generation, comprehensive visual editor, alignment/reputation simulation, aging degradation, advanced item crafting/enchant/recharge interfaces, controller/mobile optimization and hundreds of spells. Recording a fullscreen browser game is the streaming baseline; integrated broadcast tools are not promised.

Release gate: a fresh party completes town→both floors→boss→town resolution; all included controls work; art manifest has no visible placeholders; all six classes and 36 spells are usable; saves restore persistent state; a clean package launches from its documented commands. Design documents are not evidence that these implementation checks have passed.

## 4. Character creation and progression
Attributes STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA. Modifier = floor((score−10)/2). Creation uses the array 16,14,13,12,10,8, assigned freely; class presets put 16 in the primary attribute. No rolling, point-buy or species bonuses in this demo. Portrait and appearance text have no statistics. Names 1–24 printable characters; trim whitespace. Twelve portraits, selectable by all classes; duplicate portraits allowed. Roster limit 24 stored characters including active members. Only tavern permits creating/deleting/recruiting/dismissing. Confirmation on deletion, never delete an active character; belongings stay with stored members. Quest items, bank and journal belong to the campaign, not a dismissed member.

Shared proficiency P=2 at levels 1–4, P=3 at level 5. Attack bonus = P + relevant attribute modifier + weapon accuracy bonus. Weapon attacks roll d20 against Defense; natural 1 misses, natural 20 hits and doubles weapon dice only. Defense=10+armor bonus+shield bonus+DEX contribution+effects; DEX contribution capped at 2 in medium armor and 0 in heavy armor. Negative DEX applies before cap. Block grants +4 Defense until the actor's next scheduled turn. No opportunity attacks or positioning submenu.

Starting/max HP = class base + CON modifier + (level−1)×max(1,class gain+CON modifier); minimum max HP 1. CON changes recompute maximum; current increases by positive difference, clamps on decreases. Base/gain: Myrmidon 14/7, Artificer 11/5, Mutant 16/8, Wizard 8/4, Priest 11/5, Witch 10/5. Casters max SP=8+2×max(0,casting modifier)+4×(level−1); noncasters 0. Wizard uses INT, Priest WIS, Witch CHA. Starting HP/SP full.

Damage = weapon dice + relevant modifier + damage bonus, minimum 1 on hit, then effects/resistance. STR for melee, DEX for missiles and Artificer signature, CON for Mutant body weapon. No fixed party frontline requirement; enemy targeting selects among living active members. Enemies can telegraph a preference (e.g. weakest), which the log reports. Default select target = previously selected living enemy, else first visible living enemy. Single cast damage/healing formulas are in the spell catalogue; direct damage spells do not require a hit roll, but applicable saves apply.

Spell DC=10+P+casting modifier. Save=d20+relevant attribute modifier; match/exceed DC succeeds; no automatic 1/20 save results. Damage save halves damage, floor; status save negates. Use DEX for area flame/lightning, CON for poison/body effects, WIS for mental curses. Resistances are percentages per physical/fire/cold/lightning/poison/arcane/holy/necrotic; apply after saves, floor, range −50% to +75%. Enemy resistances are shown after first encounter; known boss immunities are described before casting. Reapplying identical buffs refreshes duration without stacking. Stronger effects win for the same statistic; independent statistics combine.

Shared earned XP pool for active adventurers: each active member receives full encounter XP, including dead members, to avoid death-driven level gaps. Stored recruits do not earn XP. Total XP thresholds for levels 2/3/4/5 are 250/750/1600/2800. Training buys one eligible level at a time for 25×new level gold; cap 5. Train raises maxima and restores the gained HP/SP only, not a free full heal. Level 3 and 5 each award +1 to a chosen attribute, cap base 18. Display pending attribute choice immediately in trainer flow.

Default primary-attribute party starts with 300 shared gold, 48 food portions, two healing draughts and two antidotes. Every member has a basic legal weapon and armor; no consumable or gold is created when recruiting a later character. New characters start at level 1 with non-sellable starter kit. Delete/recreate cannot generate money or supplies.

## 5. Class contract
Myrmidon: all weapons/armor/shields. Level 1 damage +1, increases +1 per trained level. Before battle choose Cleave (on kill, excess damage to next enemy, no recursive cleave), Crush (+2 damage, −1 accuracy), or Guard (+2 Defense). Default Cleave. Level 5 critical range remains 20; add one weapon die to criticals. No Stunt Dice pool.
Artificer: light/medium armor, shields, simple weapons/crossbows and signature devices. Signature shot d6+DEX, arcane, unlimited, usable as normal Attack at melee; exploration volley requires equipped ranged weapon. Level 3 signature d8; level 5 d10. Automatic lock/trap check bonus +3. Choose arcane spark or physical rivet at tavern; no gadget crafting budget. Ordinary consumable gadgets use Item.
Mutant: light/medium armor, no shield, weapons optional. Body attack d8+CON physical; d10 at level 3, d12 at 5. Choose one adaptation at tavern/rest: Venom (once/round on hit, poison 2 for 2 ticks, CON save), Carapace (+2 Defense), or Recovery (heal 1 HP after a surviving victorious combat, 2 at level 5). Default Carapace. No harvesting/assimilation minigame.
Wizard: light armor, no shield; staff/dagger; INT spells. Priest: light/medium armor, shield; mace/staff; WIS spells. Witch: light armor, no shield; staff/dagger; CHA spells. All caster weapons remain valid fallback attacks. Lists unlock via class sellers and minimum level, not automatic preparation menus. Each starts knowing the first three list entries. Known spells never expire. Later spells may all be purchased by level 5 with sufficient gold.

## 6. World clock, hazards and combat loop
Clock starts day 1, 08:00. Seven named days may use Day 1–7 labels; 28-day month, twelve months; calendar has no simulated aging penalties. Successful walk/strafe/backstep = 1 minute. Turns in place, blocked steps, menus and canceled actions = 0. Committed exploration cast/use/bash/lock attempt/interact = 1 minute unless an entry declares otherwise. Combat round = 1 minute, advanced once at round end. Moving into a trigger and its automatic descriptive text together consume only the movement minute. Travel transition costs the initiating step only. No real-time enemy attacks while reading.

Enemy detection is authored encounter entities: party commands trigger one enemy tile advance along passable cells if line of sight within 3, otherwise stay at guard position; no patrol while UI is open. Doors block sight. On entering melee distance (adjacent tile), lock movement and enter combat. Exploration Shoot fires each conscious equipped missile user once against the nearest unobstructed encounter within 3 forward tiles, then advances 1 minute and resolves one enemy approach tick; no return melee until contact. Ranged-capable enemies may retaliate once during that tick. Spells use declared sight/range and the same command cost. No repeated free volleys without enemy response.

Combat round order: descending DEX modifier+P for heroes, authored initiative for enemies; stable ties by party slot/entity ID. No initiative rolls. One action per living conscious combatant. Attack/Cast/Use/Block/Run commit one action; choosing targets, browsing, readying spells and opening inventory are free. Equip/remove in combat each commit one action; transfer/discard, roster and style/adaptation changes disabled in combat. Invalid action/cancel costs nothing. Consumables and SP are deducted atomically when a valid action commits. Enemy animation does not accept extra player actions.

Quick Fight F commits only the current actor's configured action. Hold F or the button repeats at most four friendly actions/second; release, Esc, modal opening, target prompt or battle end stops repetition. Stop on death of a hero and show feedback before continuing. Presets Attack, Readied Spell, Block, Run; spell auto-spending off by default and explicitly enabled per character. Invalid/out-of-SP spell falls back to normal Attack on a living target, else Block. No recursive prompts. Successful Run moves the whole party, a deliberate simplification of Xeen's individual flight: chance clamp(50+5×(best conscious DEX modifier−highest enemy DEX modifier),20,90) percent; success exits to recorded pre-encounter safe tile, costs 5 minutes, prevents immediate retrigger until next movement; failure spends acting turn. Boss cannot be fled once its final chamber seal closes; warn before entering, and emergency recall remains available.

At 0 HP: dead for demo purposes, cannot act/receive ordinary healing; no death-save subsystem. Cure does not resurrect. Defeat means no living active member: Load, Restart at town (lose 25% carried gold, keep bank/items/XP, all active restored), or Main Menu. Restart clears current battle and returns enemies to their pre-battle HP/state, never grants rewards. Victory applies XP/loot once using encounter completion ID. If bags full, loot remains in a persistent recoverable cache on encounter tile.

Food is individual portions; no carry-weight simulation. At each crossed 8-hour boundary consume one portion per living active member. A rest explicitly consumes that same interval's portions once, not twice. Insufficient food: resting refused; travel still allowed, but afflicted Hungry heroes deal −1 damage and cannot naturally restore SP; buying/eating one ration per living member clears hunger until next boundary. Town inn rest costs 12 gold plus food, advances 8 hours, restores living members HP/SP and cures sleep/drunk/fear; safe. Dungeon rest allowed only designated cleared rest alcoves; same food/time, no inn charge; scripted ambush only if specifically telegraphed, not random save-scumming. Temple handles poison, curse and death.

Conditions: poisoned (2 damage each combat end-round; 2 each 5 exploration minutes; never tick twice for same minute), asleep (skip next turn, damage wakes), frightened (−2 attack for 3 rounds), cursed (−2 Defense until removed), drunk (−2 accuracy for 30 minutes), hungry, dead. Resist/refresh rules explicit; poison does not stack, remaining duration 3 ticks. Living cannot receive regeneration after death. Default portraits: dead overrides afflicted overrides hurt (<50% HP) overrides normal. No unconscious separate portrait requirement.

Darkness begins in dungeon tiles without authored illumination; without party light, −2 accuracy, view distance 1, map recording continues on visited tiles. Torch or Light supplies 60 minutes; replacements refresh rather than stack. Dawn at 06:00 changes sky/audio only; spell durations are elapsed minutes or rounds, never silently reset at dawn.

Locks/traps: show Who Will; d20+DEX modifier+Artificer bonus vs authored DC. Failure consumes 1 minute and either triggers trap or marks lock failed for that character until leaving/re-entering map; no indefinite free retry. Bash uses first two living members' STR modifiers + d20 vs DC, failure deals each 1 damage (minimum HP 1 outside combat); after two failures offer alternate route clue. Mandatory locks/puzzles always have a key, alternate route or nonclass solution. Secrets always have an inspectable clue; discovery buffs highlight it, no required class. Fountains declare target/cost/use limit; single-use flags persist.

## 7. Inventory, economy and services
Each character has four categories, nine entries each: Weapons, Armor, Accessories, Misc. Equipped items count. Identical consumables stack to 9; equipment does not stack. Slots melee, ranged, body, offhand, accessory A/B. Two-handed melee conflicts with shield; separate ranged equipment allowed. Unique accessory effects do not duplicate. Noncasters may use consumable magic items. Base sale price floor(40% base value); unidentified items sell for 5 gold; starter gear 0 and cannot sell. Ordinary item properties hidden until identified except base type. Broken gear loses bonuses and attacks are unavailable, repair restores; no random break chance in demo, authored hazards only. Cursed gear cannot remove/transfer until temple uncurse. Discard requires Yes/No; quest objects cannot discard or sell. No ordinary ground-drop persistence: Discard destroys, while unclaimed loot caches remain recoverable.

Shared gold and food; gems retained as sellable treasure items, no separate spell currency. Bank gold only, no interest in short demo; explain this deliberate replacement. Banking embedded with tavern keeper. Fixed merchant stock plus unlimited basic food/torches; equipment cannot be repeatedly bought at profit. Bank gold cannot be spent until withdrawn. Check purse/capacity/eligibility before any mutation; shop transactions and saves cannot duplicate items.

Eight new NPCs/scenes: Tessa Bell (tavern/roster/bank), Orro Vane (merchant/identify/repair), Sister Vale (temple), Captain Brann (trainer), Pell Glass (Wizard seller), Nera Salt (Priest seller), Aunt Vell (Witch seller), Iona Reed (quest giver/cistern keeper). Unique service identities, no requirement for eight separate animation rigs. Spell sellers have no membership prerequisite; class and level govern eligibility.

Tavern: Drink (1 gold, drunk), Food (1 gold/portion, amount picker), Tip (2 gold; one distinct clue until exhausted), Rumors (free known rumors), Sign In, Rest, Bank, Exit. Bank Deposit/Withdraw→Gold→amount; amount integer 1..available, cancel free. Merchant Browse/Exit; category lists plus Buy/Sell/Identify/Repair/Exit, quotes shown. Identify 10/item, Repair max(1,ceil(base price×0.2)). Temple Heal costs 1 gold per missing HP, cure 10/condition, revive 50×level with full HP and 0 SP, uncurse 25/item, Donate 10 grants +1 Defense for 60 minutes, no stacking. Heal menu itemized, never hides revival cost. Trainer Train/Exit with preview and level cap. Guild Buy Spells/Spell Info/Exit. Tavern entry autosaves before roster mutations; manual save allowed in safe exploration, not mid-combat/transaction/prompt. Autosave also after resolved quest/travel, never overwrite before confirming imported saves.

## 8. Screen geometry and interaction contract
Logical 640×400 canvas (8:5), scaled with nearest-neighbor and letterboxing, no stretch. Rectangles [x,y,w,h]: viewport [8,8,440,280]; right context [456,8,176,136]; command grid [456,152,176,136]; portrait strip [8,304,440,88]; direction pad [456,304,176,88]. Remaining gaps hold frame/gem/status ornaments. Map may overlay viewport; character/inventory panels overlay upper region, preserving portrait access. Final art follows these hit areas; supported desktop minimum display 640×400 CSS pixels. At smaller windows show fit/zoom guidance rather than silently omit controls. Phase 20 may tune art resolution only if hit maps and saved UI preferences migrate; no gameplay save coordinate dependence.

Every action has a visible label/tooltip and shortcut. Topmost panel owns keys; no world hotkey leaks. F1–F6 switches character except Who Will/transfer prompts where it selects recipient; in combat it can inspect others but cannot transfer turn ownership. Esc closes top panel/cancels without spending action; never triggers flight. Enter confirms highlighted choice; arrows navigate lists only while focused in a modal. Mouse release required before repeated destructive action. Disabled control displays reason. Tooltips and UI strings are content, not baked into artwork.

Exploration command grid rows: Shoot S / Cast C / Rest R; Bash B / Dismiss D / Quests V; Automap M / Information I / Quick Reference Q. Dismiss outside tavern displays 'Manage the party at the tavern' and consumes nothing. No silent teleport or loss of character.
Combat rows: Quick Fight F / Cast C / Attack A; Use U / Run R / Block B; Quick Options O / Information I / Quick Reference Q. In noncaster turns Cast shows reason. Target pane shows maximum three enemies; encounters cap at three active enemies, reserve waves enter at round boundary, total up to six except boss summons capped by three visible. Keys 1–3 select visible enemy. Waves do not grant rewards until encounter ends. Movement pad remains visible but disabled in melee with reason 'Use Run to retreat'.
Direction pad: top Turn Left [Left], Forward [Up], Turn Right [Right]; bottom Strafe Left [Ctrl+Left], Back [Down], Strafe Right [Ctrl+Right]. Optional WASD is deferred, do not overwrite shortcut meanings. Space/click eligible near object interacts with current tile or directly facing adjacent door/NPC; no click-to-walk. Distinct objects use a short choice list, never guess silently. Interaction priority in shared tile: explicit chosen object, unresolved mandatory trigger, then visible object. Clicking distant scenery only displays name/hint.

Character sheet: six clickable abilities, level, HP/SP, Defense, resistances, XP/training threshold, conditions, class abilities, awards, age as cosmetic text, pooled gold/food and bank total. Buttons Items I / Quick Q / Exchange E / Exit Esc. Exchange swaps selected slot with F1–F6 outside combat only. Skills replaced by Class Abilities; seven Xeen attributes replaced by six Hexmaster ones; Gems readout replaced by Supplies.
Inventory: Weapons W / Armor A / Accessories C / Misc M / Equip E or Use U / Remove R / Discard D / Quest Q / Exit Esc. Rows 1–9 select. Transfer T enters recipient prompt F1–F6 (explicit mode avoids conflicting inspect semantics), disabled combat. Shop shell: categories W/A/C/M, Buy B / Sell S / Identify I / Fix F / Exit Esc. Owner/buyer selection retained; preview bonuses and price. Sell switches list to owned items, Buy switches vendor stock, never infers ownership from row index.
Cast panel: caster, SP available, readied spell, cost, range, valid contexts; Cast C / New N / Exit Esc. New lists known spells, selectable via click/Up/Down/Enter; selecting readies but does not spend a turn. Party target auto-confirms; single member uses F keys; enemy uses 1–3; world spells preview destination/object with explicit confirm. Spell selection cancelled returns to previous panel with prior ready spell unchanged until selection commits.
Map M: visited cells, walls/doors, current coordinates/facing, discovered stairs/secrets; no undiscovered quest GPS. Town map records automatically; no Cartographer skill tax. Right pane toggles logo/visited minimap with '=' or click. Detection spell overlays nearby geometry without setting visited flags. Journal V: Quest Items / Current Quests / Auto Notes / Up / Down / Exit; completed quests accessible by filter. Information I shows clock/date, lights, conditions, protections/durations. Quick Reference Q shows every member HP/SP/level/condition and party gold/food/bank.
Control Panel Tab or centre gem: Effects E / Music M / Load L / Save S / Save As A / Main Menu Q / Emergency Recall W / Help H / Exit Esc. Extra visible controls for fullscreen, scale, reduce motion and volume; recall confirms penalty: 25% carried gold, at least 10 if available (never negative), 60 minutes, returns to tavern with surviving members unchanged, no free revival. Available during combat via panel; restores encounter pre-battle state, no loot/XP. Main Menu confirms if unsaved. Audio begins after user gesture. Tutorial Help is separate from recall.
Ornaments: centre compass; four gems signal fire/cold/lightning/poison protection; small book reacts to nearby secret when detection active; mirror pulses on detected danger; wing ornament marks levitation-style protection if later spell data uses it. Ornament has tooltip/text equivalent; unused indicators stay inert. Portrait click opens sheet unless a target prompt owns it; health marker green ≥75%, yellow 25–74%, red 1–24%, blue dead; actor highlight distinct from inspected character highlight.
Generic prompts: Yes/No Y/N, Who Will F1–F6, integer amounts, 1–24-character password with case-insensitive trimmed match, Up/Down scrolling, Enter accept, Esc cancel. Always show cost and consequence before committing. Opening/closing any UI preserves selected target and actor.

## 9. Story and authored-map contract — all new demo lore
Bellwether Quay's water rings like struck glass. Below town, a forgotten resonator is waking machinery and living things into hostile forms. Iona asks the party to retrieve the regulator; what appears to be a monster hunt becomes a decision about repairing a dangerous but useful town utility.
Coordinates x/y 0–15, origin northwest; north decreases y. Solid perimeter except declared exits. Town spawn (7,12), facing N; tavern entrance (4,11), shop (10,11), temple (4,6), trainer (11,6), Wizard seller (2,3), Priest seller (6,3), Witch seller (10,3), Iona (8,8), dungeon stair (13,2). Street route links every service; no combat in town. Shops are scene overlays anchored to map entrances, not extra maps. Town fountain (7,7): one free full living HP/SP refill per campaign, explicit prompt. Main quest never requires its use.
Upper Cistern entrance (2,13); primary route to pump hall (7,9), regulator junction (11,7), stair down (13,2); loop via west reservoir (3,5), rest alcove (2,9), secret room (5,2). Mandatory locked gate has recovered key and bash/alternate corridor solutions. Optional trapped side cache and one light-sensitive clue; torch also solves clue. Narrative clues are written to Auto Notes.
Resonator Vault entrance (2,13); rest alcove (3,11), three-valve puzzle (7,8), bypass corridor (11,10), boss threshold (12,4), boss arena (12,2), regulator (13,2). Valve order is discoverable on level one and repeated in this floor's maintenance note. No softlock from wrong order; wrong entry vents a bounded damage hazard (never takes a living hero below 1) and resets. Shortcut back to upper floor opens after regulator. Phase 17/18 authors complete cell layouts consistent with these anchors; Phase 1 does not pretend final tile maps exist.
Main stages: MQ01 hear Iona's request; MQ02 obtain cistern key and enter; MQ03 learn regulator sequence and descend; MQ04 reset valves/open chamber; MQ05 defeat Bell-Heart Custodian and secure regulator; MQ06 return to Iona, choose repair or disconnect, receive ending/reward. Repair/disconnect are narrative ending flags with different epilogue, same completion reward. Neither deletes access to optional content. Quest state monotonic; item pickup and objective update atomic.
Optional: SQ01 Tessa's lost ledger (upper secret); SQ02 Orro's missing tool roll (trapped side chamber); SQ03 Nera's bottled echo (vault side shrine, peaceful interaction or enemy route). Each has one turn-in and one reward, no repeat XP. Critical key/notes/regulator stored outside bags. Clearing boss does not lock out turn-ins. No race/class-specific mandatory solution.

Eight ordinary enemy concepts: Cistern Rat (fast weak swarm), Sludge Leech (poison), Rust Pilgrim (armored humanoid construct), Lantern Moth (lightning), Pipe Goblin (ranged bolts), Glass Slime (physical resistance), Bell Wraith (fear/necrotic), Valve Sentinel (slow heavy). Boss Bell-Heart Custodian alternates a telegraphed resonant strike and vulnerable recovery; no counter requires a particular class. Every enemy definition declares HP/Defense/initiative/attacks/resistances/XP/loot and animation IDs. Exact individual numbers authored and encounter-tested in Phase 7/17/18 using party formulas here; changing global formulas requires versioned design note.

## 10. Content and implementation data contract
Stable IDs are lowercase ASCII snake_case with prefixes map_, npc_, enemy_, item_, spell_, quest_, flag_, asset_. Never reuse a removed ID. Definitions immutable; mutable state separate. Save root contains schemaVersion, contentVersion, campaignId, seededRngState, clockMinutes, partyIds, roster, sharedResources, bankGold, mapStates, questStates, discoveredNotes, settings and pendingLoot. Character contains ID/class/name/portrait/baseAttributes/level/XP/HP/SP/inventory/equipment/conditions/knownSpellIds/readiedSpellId/quickAction/style. Map state stores tile position/facing, discovered cells, door/object states and encounter completion flags. Runtime animation frames are not saved.
Spell schema: id, classId, name, minLevel, price, spCost, contexts, targetType, rangeTiles, lineOfSight, effects[], durationUnit/value, saveAttribute, onSave, stackGroup, environmentalTags, animationId, soundId, description. Null save means no save. Definition validation rejects missing effect/target/visual keys and class-list duplication. Catalogue supplies exact initial magnitudes; area targets means all active enemies (max three), not reserve waves.
Item schema: id/name/category/slot/allowedClasses/basePrice/stackLimit/weaponDice/damageType/accuracyBonus/armorBonus/effects/charges/curse/identifiedDefault/iconId. Instances carry unique ID, definition ID, count, charges, identified, broken, cursed and starter flag. Quest state is explicit stage plus claimed reward flag; script triggers are idempotent.
Event sequence: validate command→compute deterministic result→atomically mutate world/resources→emit presentation events→advance time/conditions/enemy response→evaluate transitions→permit next input. Never let animation callbacks grant loot or decide combat outcomes. Saves at stable command boundaries only; validate imports against size/count limits, known IDs and schema, never execute imported code. Keep original save copy before migration. Three manual slots plus one rotating autosave; import creates new manual slot after confirmation. Browser private-mode/storage failure offers export and a clear message.
Architecture for Phase 2: client presentation, shared pure rules, content definitions, Node static server. No multiplayer protocol or database. Pin actual dependencies then, not in this design phase. Package includes start/build/check commands and project-state handoff. No hosted site is created by Phase 1.

## 11. Art, audio and acceptance
See Asset_Manifest.csv for production IDs and ownership. Three environment kits need floors, front/side walls, corners, doors open/closed/locked, distance masks, stairs and props. Renderer establishes depth slots 0–3 and lateral slots before final painting. Source art masters separate from indexed sprite sheets; atlas trim metadata preserves anchors. Pixel palette and dithering consistent; no blurry resizing or generic vector placeholders in final build. Town saturated and welcoming; cistern green/amber; vault violet/brass. Exaggerated silhouettes and expressive monster anticipation/hit/death, rather than only bobbing sprites.
Final NPC scenes have face/blink/gesture loops and scene accents. Reuse animation rigs where visually appropriate but all eight scenes distinct. Actor proof in Phase 20 establishes actual frame dimensions/footprint before batches. Twelve portraits × four treatments = 48 rendered states, treatment overlays allowed. Spell art uses eight shared families with variations across 36 entries. Forty ordinary item icons plus eight reward icons may use readable category families while remaining identifiable.
Audio: three looping tracks (town, dungeon, combat), boss variation, title/ending cue, three ambience beds, reusable UI/movement/weapon/spell/condition/monster cues. Credits record source/license/author/generation provenance as applicable; no Xeen assets included. Music/SFX independent volume, mute and persisted preferences. Art and audio validation checks actual integrated scenes, not only existence of files.

## 12. Acceptance and handoff
Phase 1 is complete when formulas/control semantics/content budget are fixed, all 36 spells have costs/effects, the coverage matrix assigns every normal control family, the asset manifest has ownership, new lore is labeled, and Phase 2 can implement the foundation without inventing new combat systems. This package was checked for catalogue counts, unique IDs, phase ranges and expected source files. It has not been playtested, rendered as a game or balanced in-engine.
Implementation checkpoints: P6 persistent movement; P14 systems foundation; P19 complete adventure with provisional art; P30 complete art coverage; P34 release. Every phase updates PROJECT_STATE.md with implemented systems, save version, verification, remaining placeholders and next phase. Preserve this specification and record deliberate revisions rather than quietly drifting.
