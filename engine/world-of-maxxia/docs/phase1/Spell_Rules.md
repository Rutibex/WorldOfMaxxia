# Spell catalogue conventions
All entries are proposed World of Maxxia adaptations. INT/WIS/CHA mean the corresponding modifier, not raw score. Healing/damage minimum 1 before saves/resistance. dN uses seeded rules RNG. Prices of zero mark starting spells; every newly created caster knows these three, without grantable scrolls. Enemy spell range is 3 tiles unobstructed ahead in exploration or the selected visible enemy in melee. Enemies plural means all active visible enemies. Party/ally spells work in combat unless restricted; combat uses one action, exploration uses one minute. Light and other exploration-only utilities cannot be cast in combat. Unspecified instant effects have no duration. A status duration starts after application and ends after the target has had the specified number of scheduled turns; player-turn effects expire immediately before that hero's next turn after N full rounds. Exploration converts one round of status to one minute. Exploration-only movement previews destination and validates all constraints before commitment. Dead allies excluded from group healing. No overheal, SP regeneration spell, item duplication, unlimited currency spell or untargetable summon entity in this demo. Same-stat buffs do not stack; Stone Skin and Sheltering Hand share defense_buff; stronger value wins. All costs are payable solely in SP. Spell catalogue balancing may change after playtesting with a version note.
