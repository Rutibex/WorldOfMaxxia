# Rendered effects prompt set

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool with transparent_background=true. Final deliverables are packed from the returned RGBA artwork. The following production specifications describe the four requests; they are not procedural drawing recipes.

1. **elemental-sequences.png:** A regular 6×6 transparent atlas of hand-painted luminous fantasy VFX. Each row is one coherent six-frame sequence: forming, growing, full strike, spreading, dissipating and remnants. Rows: orange-red fire, cyan frost shards, violet arcane comet, yellow-blue lightning, green poison vapor, purple-black necrotic wisp. Require consistent centers, generous padding and no labels, borders, panels, people or UI symbols.
2. **support-sequences.png:** A 6×6 transparent atlas with the same six-step development. Rows: emerald-gold healing leaf motes, golden holy sunlight, cyan guard barrier, silver-amber physical sparks, magenta binding threads and violet-blue dimensional portal. No physical objects, icon frames or unrelated symbol substitutions. Only healing/binding/portal rows are used in the final pack; the other rows were superseded for cleaner gutters.
3. **status-arrow-starfall.png:** A 6-column, 3-row transparent atlas. First row: poison droplet, purple sleepy Zs, frightened ghost, cursed eye, ale tankard and bread loaf. Second row: six frames of the same upward-pointing steel arrow with airflow trails, stable pose and center. Third row: coherent silver-blue starfall buildup, strike and fading sparks. Detailed painted raster shading, no backing rectangles or labels.
4. **clean-impact-sequences.png:** A wide 2:1 atlas, exactly six columns by three equal rows. Small centered sprites with huge clear transparent gutters; no text, backgrounds, borders or icon frames. Each row is six frames from tiny buildup through peak and breakup to fading motes. Rows: pure golden-white sunlight burst with no leaves/symbols; cyan magic barrier catching a glint and dissolving; silver diagonal impact glint bursting into amber sparks. This replaces the three crowded support rows.
