# Ground art

Original corpse art generated using built-in image_gen: a low pile of weathered dark leather armor, muted purple cloak, horned monster skull and belt pouch, painted fantasy raster game art, no gore, no floor plane, transparent background. Front three-quarter view, soft overhead lighting, readable at128×128.

Original:corpse-original.png. Production:public/assets/art/ground/corpse.png, alpha-trimmed and resized to128×128 without code-painted artwork. One generic remains sprite is reused across named monster corpses. Loose items use existing illustrated item sprites, and plot pickups reuse existing scenery art.
